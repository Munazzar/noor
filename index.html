How to add the widgets

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → Noor. Drag Prayer Times, Prayer Times (row), Daily Qur'an & Sunnah, My Reminders, Qur'an Reader or Noor Dock onto the screen, then resize it. Noor Dock opens the dock clock with one tap; you can also long-press the Noor icon → Dock.

Qur'an Reader shows one verse at a time. Read ✓ (or tapping the right half) marks the verse as read and moves to the next one. Your progress is shared with the Read tab here.

On a widget, tap the right half of the card (or →) to slide to the next card, and tap the left half (or ←) to go back. ↗ opens the card here in full. Android doesn't let widgets respond to sideways finger swipes, because those are reserved for moving between home screens.

Sources

Qur'an (all 6,236 verses in the Read tab, plus the daily cards): Uthmani Arabic text with the Saheeh International English translation (via the quran-json dataset from The Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia). Verse numbers are standard.

Sunnah: about 500 hadith, all from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim. Most are the narrations Imam an-Nawawi collected in Riyad as-Salihin, taken from the sunnah.com database, with the same numbering and source.

In Times Of…: verses from the same Qur'an text; hadith only from Bukhari & Muslim or graded sahih/hasan (al-Albani, Darussalam), copied from the sunnah.com database; Tafsir Ibn Kathir from quran.com; scholar sayings only with a book reference.

Prayer times are calculated on your phone with the open-source Adhan library (MIT licence). The offline city list is from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Adhan recordings are downloaded from a public collection on GitHub only when you choose one.

Live content comes from api.alquran.cloud (Qur'an) and the open hadith-api (Bukhari & Muslim only). It's labelled NEW. Tap any card to open its source.

Bukhari & Muslim in the Library: the complete Sahih al-Bukhari (7,554 hadith) and Sahih Muslim (7,360), Arabic with English, from the open hadith-api (fawazahmed0), numbered as on sunnah.com.

Adhkar & Ruqyah: morning and evening adhkar from the Morning-and-Evening-Adhkar-DB (MIT), which follows Hisn al-Muslim with each source and grading; one narration graded weak there is left out. The other adhkar and the ruqyah are only from the Qur'an, Bukhari and Muslim, or narrations graded sahih or hasan, each shown with its reference. References to Bukhari or Muslim open the full hadith in the app.

Recitations stream from everyayah.com (verse by verse) and mp3quran.net (whole surahs, with their verse timings), and are saved on the phone only if you download them. Tajweed colours: rule positions from the open quran-tajweed data by Collin Fair (CC BY 4.0). Madinah page numbers: the quran-meta package (MIT).

Fonts: Amiri Quran, Inter, Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Dancing Script, Great Vibes, Caveat, Quicksand, Bebas Neue and DSEG7, all under the SIL Open Font License.
